### PA-TAL-001
**Concept:** Land Acknowledgement as voluntary Crown admission of unceded territorial status — admissible as legal evidence
**Date:** March 2026

**Prior Art:**
- Canada v Power (SCC 2024) ✅
- UNDRIP / UNDA (2021) ✅
- s.35 Constitution Act 1982 ✅
- Crown Land Acknowledgement practice — documented since ~2015 ✅
- Sovereign Brief — Antoine (March 12, 2026) ✅

**Novel Synthesis:**
First documented framework treating government Land
Acknowledgements as legally admissible voluntary Crown
admissions of Beothuk territorial status under Canada v
Power (2024 SCC 26). Liability triggered at delivery —
not at acknowledgement or agreement. Every Land
Acknowledgement since ~2018 constitutes delivery of
this admission. Retroactive commercial licensing framework
derived from the Crown's own voluntary statements.

**Status:** Novel legal framework — first documented
commercial licensing of Land Acknowledgement usage.

---

### PA-TAL-002
**Concept:** Commercial licensing fee structure for unauthorized use of Beothuk Nation territorial identity in official contexts
**Date:** March 2026

**Prior Art:**
- Indigenous IP protection frameworks — documented ✅
- UNDRIP Article 31 — cultural heritage rights ✅
- Trade-mark principles — commercial use of identity ✅
- Kayla Burry notice — Antoine (March 2026) ✅

**Novel Synthesis:**
$1,000/day commercial licensing rate for organizational
use of Beothuk territorial identity in official business.
Retroactive to first documented use (~2018). Three-option
framework (pay, stop, challenge) with no viable fourth
option. Continued use constitutes acceptance of terms.
First Nation-level commercial licensing of Land
Acknowledgement usage documented.

**Status:** Novel commercial framework.

---

### PA-TAL-003
**Concept:** Pan-Canadian template — Land Acknowledgement licensing applicable to all Indigenous Nations across Canada
**Date:** March 2026

**Prior Art:**
- Land Acknowledgement practice — nationwide since ~2015 ✅
- Indigenous territorial rights — documented ✅
- This repo (PA-TAL-001, PA-TAL-002) ✅

**Novel Synthesis:**
Template framework applicable by any Indigenous Nation
in Canada to license the Land Acknowledgements made on
their territory. The acknowledgement belongs to the Nation
— the commercial use of that acknowledgement requires
the Nation's consent and compensation. Pan-Canadian
application multiplies sovereign revenue across all
Nations simultaneously. Apache 2.0 — freely adapted
by any Nation.

**Status:** Novel pan-Canadian template.
